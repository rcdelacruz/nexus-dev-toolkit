nexus v4 — preview (pre-release)

nexus for Product Owners and BAs

You decide what gets built and why. /brd and /prd turn meetings, notes and client documents into a Business Requirements Document and a Product Requirements Document with stable IDs — and every later step (architecture, designs, dev tasks, change requests) traces back to them.

Preview. nexus v4 isn't on PyPI yet. This page runs it as nexus4, next to your nexus 3, which stays exactly as it is. Set it up with one line: Set Up Once.

Who This Is For

Product owners, business analysts and anyone who writes requirements. You work only in docs/requirements/; you commit with /commit and open the pull request with /create-pr — each shows you what it will do and waits for your yes; nothing is committed or pushed before you've reviewed it. New to git? Read the git guide. A complete worked example is in the clinic appointments example.

Set Up Once

$ alias nexus4="uvx --refresh --from 'git+https://github.com/rcdelacruz/nexus-dev-toolkit@v4-preview' nexus"   # the nexus 4 preview
$ nexus4 init . --role product   # /brd, /prd, /requirements, /change-request — no developer tools

nexus4 is the preview under its own name: your nexus (nexus 3) and your other projects stay as they are, and every run picks up the newest preview by itself. To keep nexus4 in new terminals, add the alias line to ~/.zshrc (or ~/.bashrc).

$ export TYPESAFE_API_KEY=<your key>   # optional: TypeSafe judgments

Optional — TypeSafe. With a key, nexus asks TypeSafe where it would otherwise guess from keywords: the sections of a doc that doesn't follow the guide, design tokens its rules can't place, and which technologies a doc really uses. Without a key, nexus uses its built-in rules and nothing is sent. Add the line to ~/.zshrc (or ~/.bashrc) and start Claude Code from that terminal. Never put the key in .mcp.json: it's shared in git.

It also adds docs/requirements/templates/BRD-TEMPLATE.md and PRD-TEMPLATE.md and the requirements guide — yours to edit; nexus never overwrites them.

Where You Fit

flowchart TB
  B["/brd · BRD.md · business rules BR-"] --> P["/prd · PRD.md · US- FR- NFR- AC-"]
  P --> A["/architect · splits the product into epics"]
  A --> D["designs · /design or a copied export (designers)"]
  D --> S["/scaffold · tech lead · Day 0, once"]
  S --> T["/dev-tasks-planner · tech lead · one CSV per epic"]
  T -.->|"later, the client asks for a change"| CR(["/change-request"])
  CR -.->|"requirements first"| P
  CR -.->|"then only the affected tasks"| T
  class B,P,A,D,S,T step
  class CR human

Every command looks first, asks you what's missing, shows a plan, stops for your approval, writes, then checks its own work against the requirements guide. Nothing is invented: what nobody has answered yet goes into the document's Open Questions with an owner.

/brd [files | "problem description"]

The business view of the whole product, in docs/requirements/BRD.md: problem, business goals, stakeholders, scope in and out, business rules (BR-001 …) and success metrics with a baseline and a target. Give it whatever you have — a problem description, a file with all the product's requirements and problems, meeting notes, a client email, an old BRD — or nothing, and it asks one short round of questions for the gaps.

> /brd "Clinics lose 18% of slots to no-shows and booking is phone-only"
> /brd ~/Downloads/product-requirements.md ~/Downloads/kickoff-notes.md

You don't split the product into modules — the architect does that next, as epics, and designs and dev tasks follow those epics.

| BR-002 | A patient can hold at most 2 future appointments at a time. | Clinic manager, kickoff |
| Reduce no-shows | 18 % | ≤ 8 % by Q3 | monthly no-show report | BR-004 |

/prd

The product view of the whole product, built from the BRD, in docs/requirements/PRD.md: personas, user stories with acceptance criteria, functional and non-functional requirements, flows and release scope. Every story and requirement says which business rule it serves, and the plan shows any rule nothing serves yet.

### US-003 — Book an appointment online
As a patient, I want to pick a free slot and book it, so that I don't have to call. (serves BR-001, BR-002)
- AC-007 Only free slots of the chosen doctor are shown.
- AC-008 A third future booking is refused with a clear message.

/requirements [source]

For documents you already have: imports an existing BRD, PRD or brief (Markdown, Word, PDF), gives every requirement an ID in the project's scheme, and moves requirement files out of docs/arch-docs/.

/change-request CR-001 "…"

When the client changes their mind, the change starts with you: update the BRD/PRD (a changed requirement keeps its ID), then nexus reads the git diff and records exactly which IDs changed, were added or removed — and lists the dev tasks that trace to them. Developers re-plan only those.

Traceability

The chain your IDs make possible:

BR-004 reduce no-shows  →  US-006 reminder before the visit  →  FR-NOTIF-001 send an SMS reminder
   →  architecture: Celery scheduled job (Choice line)  →  screen DES-004  →  task NOTIF-002

Change BR-004 and nexus can name every story, screen and task that depends on it.
