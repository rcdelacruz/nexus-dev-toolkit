# Kickoff workshop — CareSlot online booking (raw notes)

*Typed during the meeting, not cleaned up. Present: clinic manager (Dr. Lina Ocampo), front-desk lead
(Joy), one doctor (Dr. Ana Reyes), data protection officer (Mark), our PO. All names made up.*

- 3 outpatient clinics, one shared schedule per doctor. Today booking is **phone only**.
- Joy: mornings are calls calls calls. Avg wait at peak ~6 min (she checked the phone system). Patients hang up.
- Lina: "biggest pain = no-shows". Says roughly 1 in 5 don't come (report attached — 18%). Doctors sit idle while
  others can't get a slot.
- Double bookings happen "a couple of times a week" — two people editing the paper-backed schedule at once.
  Joy: "it has to be impossible, not just rare. A patient turned up and someone was already in the room."
- Lina wants patients to book online **any time of day**, up to **30 days ahead** (beyond that the doctors'
  hours aren't fixed yet).
- Lina: some patients book 4–5 slots "just in case" then don't come. Rule: **max 2 future appointments per
  patient**. Agreed by all.
- Cancelling: patients should cancel online themselves, but **not in the last 2 hours** — too late to give the
  slot to someone else; then they call the desk. (Lina)
- Reminders: Lina wants **everyone reminded before every appointment** — she thinks most no-shows simply forget.
  She said "the day before" (we didn't agree on timing — follow up?).
- Patients see their own upcoming appointments.
- Front desk needs to see and fix every doctor's day: book for walk-ins/phone callers, move, cancel.
- Dr. Reyes: doctors want to see their own day in the morning. Read-only is fine.
- Slots are 20 min, inside each doctor's weekly working hours.
- Mark (DPO): patient data **must stay in our region**, only the patient and clinic staff may see it, and he
  wants a log of who looked at patient data. Also: people must sign in — email + password is OK for now.
- Mark: lock the account after a few wrong passwords. Suggested 5 tries / 15 min.
- Mark: log people out when idle — "30 minutes, like the hospital system".
- NOT now: payments/insurance (later), video consults (no plans), link to the hospital's medical records (release 2).
- Deadline: live **before flu season — 1 October**. Team: 2 developers + 1 designer.
- Open: should walk-in patients go into the online schedule? Joy will check with the team.
- Lina, targets (she'll confirm in writing): see the report.
