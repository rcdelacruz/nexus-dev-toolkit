# Interview — front desk (Joy, front-desk lead) — raw notes

**How does a booking work today?**
A patient calls, we look at the doctor's day on screen (a spreadsheet, printed every morning), find a free 20-minute
slot, write the name in. Two of us on the phones. If both of us take the same slot at the same time, nobody notices
until the patient arrives.

**What do you need from the new system?**
- One screen per day showing every doctor's appointments.
- Book for patients who call or walk in, move an appointment to another time, cancel.
- The same protection as online: if a slot is taken, it's taken — for us too.
- When a day is empty, say so; don't show a blank grid.

**What do doctors need?**
Their own list for today: time, patient name, what the visit is for. They don't change anything.

**What should patients be able to do themselves?**
See free times, book, see their bookings, cancel (not at the last minute — we need time to fill the slot).
Most of them will use their phones.

**Anything that worries you?**
"People booking five slots to be safe and then not coming." And patients giving the wrong phone number — the
reminders won't reach them.
